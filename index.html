Getting started

Quick guide
  1. Enter your WhatsApp number with the correct country selected.
  2. Generate a pairing code and wait for it to appear.
  3. Open WhatsApp on your phone → Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number instead.
  4. Enter the code shown by Alpha-XMD.
  5. Toggle Christmas Mode and your auto-features in Bot settings.
