Quick instructions for connecting your WhatsApp.

How to pair your device

1. Enter your phone number using the country selector.
2. Generate a pairing code.
3. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
4. Go to Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number instead.
5. Enter the code shown on this dashboard.

Dashboard access

The browser stores a private control token when a new pairing is created. Keep your browser data safe. If you clear it, you may lose access to account settings and disconnect controls.
