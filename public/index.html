What is Alpha-XMD?

Alpha-XMD is a free, feature-rich WhatsApp bot developed and powered by Alpha. It connects to your WhatsApp account through the Linked Devices pairing process, bringing useful AI, media, group, and channel tools directly into your chats.

01 • AI and Creation

AI chat and creative tools

Ask AI assistants questions, generate images, and use creative tools right in your chats.

02 • Media and Utilities

Downloads and everyday media tools

Search and download from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest. Create stickers instantly.

03 • Groups and Channels

WhatsApp group and channel tools

Moderate group links, manage members, auto-react to channel posts, and secure your community.
